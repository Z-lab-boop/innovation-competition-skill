# Judging alignment and submission QC

## Rubric matrix

Create one row per verified judging item:

`rubric item | weight | intended claim | evidence ID | plan section | slide | speaker answer | status/gap`

Prioritize missing high-weight evidence before rewriting low-weight prose. If official weights are unavailable, label priorities as provisional.

## Consistency audit

Check the same facts across the application, plan, deck, spreadsheet, poster, demo, evidence appendix, and defense answers:

- Project and product names, track, team, and organization
- Technical capabilities, maturity, test conditions, and limitations
- Patent/copyright status and ownership
- User, pilot, partner, contract, order, revenue, and award counts
- Market size, price, cost, forecast period, and funding/resource ask
- Milestones, dates, responsibilities, and current-versus-future language

Any mismatch is a blocker until one authoritative value is selected and all dependent materials are updated.

## Submission checks

- Official template, filename, format, page/slide count, file size, anonymity, and deadline
- Fonts, images, charts, videos, links, embedded media, and offline demo behavior
- Editable source plus exported submission copy
- Spelling, citations, permissions, privacy redaction, and evidence legibility
- PPT/PDF/DOCX/XLSX opens correctly and renders without clipping or substitution
- Timed defense fits the limit with a recovery margin

## Final status labels

- **Ready:** official rules verified, hard gates passed, requested files validated.
- **Conditionally ready:** usable draft exists, but named assumptions or approvals remain.
- **Blocked:** eligibility, template, evidence, permission, or file validation can cause rejection.

Report concrete blockers and owners. Never translate an internal score, automated review, or polished design into a prediction of winning.
